Case Study • Fitness Tech

MyLera App

A hyper-personalized fitness and wellness ecosystem leveraging generative AI to bridge the gap between static workout plans and dynamic human health data.

React Native Firebase GPT-4 API Redux Toolkit

The Challenge

Most fitness applications offer "one-size-fits-all" routines that fail to adapt to a user's real-time physiological state, leading to plateaued progress or injury. The market lacked a solution that could synthesize sleep data, heart rate variability, and nutritional intake into actionable, conversational coaching.
